# Introduction to Spatial Analysis and Cartography with R

Kim Antunez (@antuki)

**Duration:** 1 hour 30 minutes

This workshop, given at the conference [The Use of R in Official Statistics - uRos2026](https://r-project.ro/conference2026.html) in Avignon, consists of two parts:
- A [learning tutorial](https://antuki.github.io/uros2026_spatialanalysis/lecture.html) also available as [slides](https://antuki.github.io/uros2026_spatialanalysis/lecture-slides.html);
- A [collection of exercises](https://antuki.github.io/uros2026_spatialanalysis/exercises.html)

## Data

### Road accidents

The materials are based on the
[annual databases of road traffic injury accidents](https://www.data.gouv.fr/fr/datasets/bases-de-donnees-annuelles-des-accidents-corporels-de-la-circulation-routiere-annees-de-2005-a-2019/), in particular the 2019 edition.
*« For each injury accident (i.e. an accident occurring on a road open to public traffic, involving at least one vehicle and having caused at least one victim requiring medical care), information describing the accident is entered by the law enforcement unit (police, gendarmerie, etc.) that intervened at the scene of the accident. These entries are collected in a form called the injury accident analysis report.
All these forms constitute the national file of road traffic injury accidents, known as the “BAAC File”, administered by the National Interministerial Road Safety Observatory "ONISR".*
*Some indicators derived from this database are [certified by the official statistics authority](https://www.onisr.securite-routiere.gouv.fr/outils-statistiques/indicateurs-labellises) (decree of 27 November 2019). »*

### Base maps

The base maps come from the [IGN](https://geoservices.ign.fr/documentation/diffusion/telechargement-donnees-libres.html#contoursiris).
